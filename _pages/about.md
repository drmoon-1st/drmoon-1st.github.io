Building AI systems that can meaningfully advance human health is what I work toward as an AI researcher. The purpose of my research is to develop AI that improves people’s lives in tangible and lasting ways, particularly through applications in surgery and medicine, took root during my research experience as an research intern at <a href='https://aidtlab-dau.github.io/'>AIDTLAB</a> under the guidance of Prof. <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=8fZ6XOoAAAAJ&hl=en'>Suk-Hwan Lee</a> and collaborating with Prof. <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=K6PQdg8AAAAJ&hl=en'>Seon-Geun Jeong</a>.

Currently a M.S. student at <a href='https://medicalai.postech.ac.kr/'>Medical AI Lab</a>, I am advised by Prof. <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=PHLTD4sAAAAJ&hl=en'>Sang Hyun Park</a>.

My research centers on addressing data scarcity in the medical domain and developing physically intelligent systems capable of understanding, simulating, and interacting with complex clinical environments. Feel free to reach out at <a href="mailto:drmoon@pusan.ac.kr">drmoon@pusan.ac.kr</a>!
